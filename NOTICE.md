## Why it is public anyway

The core promise of this app is that **your artwork never leaves your browser**.
Uploaded images, saved projects, and preview results stay local unless you
download a file or explicitly opt into AI cleanup. Optional AI routes through a
server-side API so provider keys never enter the browser bundle.

That is a claim you should not have to take on faith. A public repository means
anyone can verify it rather than trust a marketing sentence. 🔍
