## Priority Issues

### [P1] Product can remain outside the initially visible mobile tool rail

Why it matters: Product is the required third workflow step, but it follows several preparation tools. A phone user may need to infer that the rail scrolls before finding the recommended destination.

Fix: In Basic mode, keep Select, the contextual preparation tool, Product, and Layers immediately visible. Move specialist tools behind a clearly labelled More action, or make Product persistent beside the workflow recommendation.

Suggested command: `$impeccable adapt`

### [P2] Product canvas interaction trails the design canvas accessibility model

Why it matters: Product artwork remains pointer operated on the canvas, its resize control is 28 pixels, and it lacks keyboard resizing. Inspector fields provide a workaround, but equivalent canvas affordances behave differently.

Fix: Add Arrow and Shift plus Arrow placement, keyboard resizing, a 44 pixel handle, and concise keyboard help.

Suggested command: `$impeccable harden`

### [P2] Export dialog targets remain compact

Why it matters: The close control is 32 pixels and primary export actions are 40 pixels high. These are high-stakes mobile actions.

Fix: Raise dialog controls to a 44 pixel minimum while retaining visible focus and clear primary-action hierarchy.

Suggested command: `$impeccable adapt`

### [P2] Canvas keyboard focus and shortcuts need stronger visible discovery

Why it matters: The keyboard behavior works, but the canvas and outer crop frame lack a distinct focus-visible treatment, and sighted keyboard users may not discover the shortcuts.

Fix: Add an inset focus ring to the canvas and crop frame, plus a compact Advanced help entry or first-focus hint.

Suggested command: `$impeccable onboard`

### [P3] Product guidance becomes dense near the final decision

Why it matters: Readiness, Print Lens, preview intent, placement shortcuts, and precision controls compete when Basic users mainly need confidence and one next action.

Fix: Lead with readiness and one recommendation in Basic mode. Collapse deeper Print Lens and precision details until requested.

Suggested command: `$impeccable distill`
